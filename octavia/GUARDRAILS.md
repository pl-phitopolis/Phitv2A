# Guardrails

Rules that change how work is done here. One line each, naming who it came from and why it
exists. Read at every session start, before any work.

A rule may be **earned** (something went wrong) or **preventative** (Albert required it, or a
named risk). Either way it carries an origin — that tag is what keeps this file from filling
with invented guesses. No incident, no instruction, no named risk means it does not go in.

Live rules are capped at ~20. Past that, triage: rules that have become permanent facts about the
code move to PROJECT.md "Careful of"; rules whose applicability has changed move to Retired with a
date. **Quiet time is not a retirement reason** — a rule nobody has tripped over may be one that is
working. Nothing is deleted.

## Live

Format, described rather than shown: a dash, the id, the origin tag in brackets, a colon, the rule
in one line, then the incident or named risk in brackets. Nothing is listed here as an example,
because a worked example in a ledger gets counted as a real entry — it has already happened twice.
